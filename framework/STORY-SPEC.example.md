# STORY SPEC — *Midnight in Manchester*  (WORKED EXAMPLE)
### This is the template filled in for the novel in this repo. Use it as a reference for the level of specificity each field needs.

---

## 1 · IDENTITY
- **Working title:** Midnight in Manchester
- **Category / genre:** New-adult romance (dual-POV, slow burn)
- **Comps:** *Off-Campus* energy meets a moody, rain-soaked British aesthetic.
- **Logline:** In her final year, an ambitious stylist lands the creative-direction break of her life — only to discover its secret architect is the swaggering academy fly-half she can't stand, who's also the ghost-producer behind half the records she loves; forced together, they must decide whether to keep performing the people their families expect or risk being who they actually are.
- **Audience / shelf:** NA romance readers who want emotional depth + a strong sense of place; promise to keep = the slow burn pays off and both leads are changed by love.

## 2 · ENGINE
- **Central dramatic question:** Will they choose to be *seen* — fully, in the daylight — or stay *safe* behind the versions they perform?
- **Theme as ARGUMENT:** *You cannot keep yourself safe by hiding the truest part of you; the only cure for the wound is to let yourself be seen by the people who stay.*
- **The conflict engine ("the catch"):** Both leads survive by performing a half-self (Ella the "strong one"; Cal the "ghost"). Being together requires being fully seen — which threatens everything they've built their safety on (her ambition/family approval, his secret/father/career). The secret is a live wire that *must* eventually get out.
- **Movements:**
  1. **Collision** — two watchers who can't be in a room meet; she's hired into his secret. *Therefore* they're bound by a deal neither can escape.
  2. **Orbit** — working proximity becomes love; the secret deepens. *But* the project gets loud and the secret becomes a liability.
  3. **Reckoning → Midnight** — the secret detonates on someone else's terms; they break; *therefore* each must stop performing and choose to be seen, separately, then together.

## 3 · VOICE
- **POV structure:** Dual close-third, alternating (Ella / Cal), past tense.
- **Register:** British, contemporary, Manchester-specific texture; high interiority; dry wit.
- **Rhythm signature:** Long rhythmic sentences punctuated by short punches.
- **Per-POV notes:**
  - **Ella** — sharp, perceptive, self-aware about performing strength; reads rooms by silhouette/palette; metaphors from styling, building, seeing.
  - **Cal** — controlled surface over a furnace; calculating; metaphors fuse **sport + music production** (the flat pass = the buried beat; "the note you don't play" = selling the gap with your hips).
- **Calibration lines:**
  - A (Ella): *"You're good at reading the odds. There's a difference."*
  - B (Cal): *"Anyone can play you a note. The trick's making them hear the one you didn't play."*

## 4 · PROTAGONISTS
› **Eleonora "Ella" Savage-Oluwa** — 21, final-year stylist/creative director.
- WANT: land the real-client project, prove styling is a viable career, win the degree show.
- NEED: to stop being the strong one alone; to let herself be looked after / be seen.
- WOUND: age 9, watched her parents do "midnight-table" money arithmetic; decided to subtract herself from the cost of the family — be the easy one forever.
- LIE: *being crushed in secret is strength; needing things makes you a burden.*
- ARC: from "carry it alone to look strong" → "set the weight down in the middle, where people who stay can help hold it."
- Signature: flinches at kindness ("kindness with an invoice"); "looking away first is an apology."

› **Callum "Cal" Vance** — 22, academy fly-half; secret ghost-producer "V".
- WANT: the senior rugby contract / his father's approval ("the nod").
- NEED: to stop being a ghost; to be one whole person in one room.
- WOUND: brother Jordan (gifted producer) died at 16; Cal left his last voice note unopened two days; father blames the music.
- LIE: *if you're already gone, you can't be left; if your name's on it, it stops being yours.*
- ARC: from "bury the true thing where it can't be taken" → "sign your own work, in the daylight, and let yourself be left-able."
- Signature: the "counting stillness"; buries Jordan's four-note melody (three climb, one falls) in every track.

## 5 · SUPPORTING CAST
- **Priya** — Ella's flatmate; the "honest mirror" B-couple with Tom; function: dramatizes being-seen vs. being-the-fun-version.
- **Tom Bryce** — rugby lock, Cal's loyal friend, Priya's boyfriend; connective tissue between worlds; runs the "who is V?" sweepstake (dramatic irony).
- **Knox (Marcus)** — rising artist Cal produces; only one who knows V=Cal; faces his own "sell out vs. stay true" choice (theme rhyme).
- **Gareth Vance** — Cal's father; ex-pro; grief-as-project; the "don't come to the house" / "where is it" arc.
- **Cal's mother** — the family's "original ghost"; keeps Jordan's room; names the pattern.
- **Sasha Reyes** — label fixer; "producer of people"; the antagonist-by-ambition who weaponises the reveal.
- **Imogen Clarke-Bayo** — London studio head; the offer; "most people never find out who they are…"
- **Margot Hale** — Ella's course rival; flawless *fictional* brand vs. Ella's risky *real* one; rival → ally.
- **Tioluwa** — Ella's 17-yr-old sister; the cleared-minefield payoff.

## 6 · SETTING
- **Places:** Manchester — Fallowfield (party), Hulme (flat above a launderette), Northern Quarter (unmarked studio), Mayfield arches (shoot + chip-shop step), Oldham Street chapel (launch), Heaton Park (Parklife). South London — Lewisham (family), Catford (mother/Jordan's room). Soho/Berwick St (Imogen).
- **Time span:** late winter → June (degree show / Parklife epilogue one year on).
- **Two worlds:** elite-university/rugby vs. the underground music/nightlife scene — and South London family vs. Manchester self.
- **Sensory bank:** fine orange streetlight in rain ("beautiful and slightly ruined"); launderette dryers' heat-and-hum through the floor; cedar; the stairwell that "smells of old paper"; wrong/expired film stock; frost burning off a dawn pitch.

## 7 · MOTIF SYSTEM
- **Jordan's four notes (three climb, one falls)** — grief buried in the music — seeded ch.1 → paid off chapel + Parklife.
- **The nod** — earned approval (Gareth's; later inverted by Ella's dad) — ch.2 → ch.5.
- **The counting stillness** — Cal's held breath — throughout.
- **Reading the room / the odds** — ch.1 → "you finally stopped reading the room" (the kiss).
- **"The catch can have everything else"** — ch.3/love → final line beat.
- **Crossing the room** — party → chapel (to father, then to Ella) → Parklife.
- **Six o'clock** — mother's daily call → the "I love you" at an ordinary hour.
- **Both names spelled right** — dad + Cal's mother → epilogue laminate.
- **Looking away first = an apology.** **Orange light / beautiful-and-slightly-ruined.** **The window vs. the door** (hiding place vs. home).

## 8 · B-PLOTS
- **Priya & Tom** — the honest, public couple; rough patch (she fears she's "the fun one") → he claims her ("twelve feet"). Rhyme: being seen vs. performing. Irony: the sweepstake.
- **The degree show & Margot** — Ella's professional stakes; real-client risk vs. flawless fiction. Rhyme: nerve vs. polish; risk of the real.
- **Knox's feature** — label pushes a commercial crossover; Knox chooses true. Rhyme: authenticity vs. selling the safe version.

## 9 · INFORMATION-STATE SEEDS
- V = Cal: known to Cal, Knox, (later) his mother, Ella (at the reveal), then Gareth + public (leak/chapel).
- Ella↔Cal relationship secret through the middle; Sasha *suspects* (toothbrush flick); goes public at the chapel.
- Jordan / the buried notes: Cal tells Ella (Jordan reveal); the film tells the *deep* story publicly **without naming him**.
- Hard rule: Ella must not act on knowledge of Jordan before the reveal; no public V=Cal before the leak.
