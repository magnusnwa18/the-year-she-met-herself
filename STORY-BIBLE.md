## CHARACTERS

### Eleanor "Nell" Hale — protagonist
- **Name/spelling:** Eleanor Hale. **Nell** to nearly everyone; **Eleanor** from Diana when serious; **El** only from Theo. Never "Ellie."
- **Age:** 34 → 35 (b. mid-October, Year −34). Architect, ~9 yrs qualified.
- **Physical anchor:** neat, mid-brown hair she keeps just-long-enough to tie back; bites the inside of her cheek before she agrees to things; straightens crooked picture frames without noticing; in any room she locates the load-bearing wall.
- **Voice signature:** the yes that lands a half-second early; self-deprecating dry wit; thinks in buildings and in rests/intervals (her mother's music).
- **Relationships:** partner Adam (4 yrs); mother Diana (carer-in-practice); father Patrick (estranged since age 9); best friend Priya (since age 11); ex Theo (together 26–28, the great love she ended); colleague/rival Cora.
- **Wound/Lie:** see spec §4. Lie = "cost no one anything, keep every door open, and no one leaves / I stay safe."
- **Knows & when:** does NOT know (a) the real reason Patrick left until **ch11**; (b) what Ellis has done in lost time until *after* each act; (c) Theo's divorce until **ch8**. Has told NO ONE about the **31-yr-old pregnancy/miscarriage** (Marcus) until she speaks it **ch11–12**.
- **Status over timeline:** with Adam at open → engagement deferred/unraveling mid → ends it honestly ch12. Sole architect at Crane & Soder → quits to start own practice ch12–13. Carer → bereaved (Diana dies ch11) → owner of the derelict house.

### The four alternate selves (ALL Eleanor — track as full characters; each is a *path*, not a person met)
> Rendered in **present tense**; narration migrates into their close-third during full glimpses. Each has ONE crisp anchor so they never blur.
- **ELLIS** — *career/London path* (took the London job at 26 instead of staying for Diana). Celebrated architect; glass-and-steel; a South Bank flat with a view and no one in it. **Anchor:** cold hands; cropped, expensively greyed hair; corrects other people's lines by reflex; speaks in the imperative. **Theme:** the impressive life; certainty as frost. **THE PREDATOR** — the self trying to take over (certainty is what's seductive to a woman drowning in choice).
- **LENI** — *love path* (stayed with Theo at 28; gave up the firm). Warm, smaller life; flour/paint; says "we" by reflex; laughs too easily. **Anchor:** paint or flour under the nails; a body held often. **Theme:** love as the wealth traded for the other life.
- **ELLE** — *freedom path* (took the sabbatical / never came back; her father's daughter). Rootless, in transit, abroad; never says "home." **Anchor:** sun-damage; a tattoo she doesn't remember getting; can't sit still. **Theme:** "leave before you're left."
- **THE MOTHER-SELF** — *family path* (the pregnancy at 31 continued). Daughter **Edie** (~age 2–3 in the visions). Exhausted-radiant, attention always halved, never alone, never rested. **Anchor:** a child's drawing in her bag; speech broken by a toddler's needs. **Theme:** the road most nearly taken — pointedly still just *Nell, with a daughter* (no new name).

### Diana Hale — mother
- 66. Former piano teacher (taught from the front room for 30 yrs). **Vascular dementia**, worsening across the year (early slips ch1 → diagnosis ch2 → care home mid → death ch11). Widowed-in-effect (Patrick left, never divorced cleanly — *provisional: legally still married; confirm ch9/11*).
- **Physical anchor:** still has a pianist's hands; hums under her breath; a specific cardigan; the flat **D** in her own piano she never had fixed ("it's honest, that note").
- **Voice:** praising, mild, deflecting — until dementia strips the deflection and she becomes startlingly direct.
- **Knows:** the true reason Patrick left (she told him to go — *provisional twist*); reveals it unguarded **ch11**. The dementia-music fact: she can still **play** Satie's Gymnopédie No.1 after she can no longer reliably name Eleanor.

### Adam Reyes — partner
- 37. Structural engineer (checks whether other people's designs stand up — deliberate rhyme with Eleanor's evasions). Kind, steady, slightly safe; cooks; plans things. Loves the easy/adaptable Nell.
- **Anchor:** rolls his sleeves to the same point each time; says "no, honestly, whatever you'd prefer" and means it (mirrors her own self-erasure back at her — they are two accommodating people, which is why nothing in the relationship is *chosen*).
- **Arc:** proposes ch1; senses her absence through the year; they release each other honestly ch12. Allowed anger and dignity. NOT a villain.

### Theo Marsh — the love given up
- 36. Was a furniture-maker / now (*provisional*) restores boats or runs a small build firm — works with his hands (rhyme: he makes real things; Eleanor only designs safe ones). Together with Eleanor 26–28; he wanted Lisbon / the leap; she chose the firm + Diana. **Recently divorced**, no children; back in Bristol from ~ch5.
- **Anchor:** sawdust; calls her **El**; a habit of finishing other people's sentences correctly, which once felt like being known and once like being decided-for.
- **Knows/reveals:** his divorce (Eleanor learns ch8); the true cost their ending had for him (ch12). Has his own near-relationship (*provisional: someone named Rae*) — he is not a waiting prize.

### Priya Nair — best friend
- 35. Married to **Sam** (*provisional name — NB avoid confusion; consider "Dev"*); two children (~6 and ~3). A&E nurse or teacher (*provisional: teacher*). The model of a fully chosen, hard life.
- **Arc:** her marriage strains badly mid-book (B1); she and Eleanor nearly break over Eleanor's drifting (ch8/10); reconcile ch13. The living argument that a chosen life is real, not easy.
- **Anchor:** always halfway through a task; tells the truth a beat too fast.

### Patrick Hale — father
- 68. Left when Eleanor was 9. Rootless since; currently runs a bar on a Greek island (*provisional: Naxos / a fictional island*). Charming, hollow, alone — the living Elle. Returns as Diana declines (ch9), reaches an imperfect honest peace, leaves again — but as a *named* goodbye, not an abandonment.
- **Anchor:** sun-cured skin; tells the same three good stories; a watch he never sets to local time.

### Dr. Ada Okafor — neurologist
- 50s. Sees Eleanor ch6 (Eleanor fears she's inheriting Diana's illness). Offers a possible organic explanation for the visions; humane; leaves both doors open. Eleanor declines further testing (ch10/12). **Never resolved** on the page.

### Cora Vance — colleague/rival
- 31. Wants the partnership at Crane & Soder nakedly. The real-world Ellis. Gets what Eleanor "lost," at the cost Ellis paid. **Anchor:** already dresses like Ellis; never makes the tea.

### Vivian "Viv" Ashe — Diana's oldest friend  *(new, intro ch2)*
- 74. Retired architect — one of very few women of her generation in the profession; built a celebrated chapel-conversion in the '80s, then "stopped, on purpose" (her phrase). Diana's friend since art school (they met at 19). Widow; no children, by choice she names without apology.
- **Function in theme:** the one person who hears Eleanor's visions and does NOT reckon her ill — because she has her *own* uncanny history she only half-explains ("I had a year like that once. You don't get told twice, so listen the first time."). She is a **living, warmer, wiser Ellis-adjacent** — a woman who chose the work and can speak honestly to what it cost and what it was worth; and a mentor/oracle distinct from Diana (Diana praised easiness; Viv has only ever asked Eleanor what she *wants*).
- **Anchor:** a draughtsman's habit of squinting one eye; always has a propelling pencil; says the unsayable thing kindly and then changes the subject.
- **Mystery she carries:** did she truly live a "year" like Eleanor's, or is it metaphor? Held ambiguous; pays off late. Also the keeper of some of Diana's truths (knew Patrick; knows about the cottage; possibly knows the ch11 reveal before Eleanor does — *information-state: Viv must not voice the Patrick truth before Diana does in ch11*).

### Julian Vane — starchitect, the man who offered London  *(intro ch3)*
- ~60s. Founder/god of London practice **Halsworth Vane**. Eight years ago he personally phoned Eleanor (then 26) to offer her a place; she said no ("my mother's not well"). Has quietly asked after "the Bristol woman, same name" every year or two since. Now **lead juror** of the open international competition for a **riverside cultural building** (his own celebrated South Bank building = the **"Tidewrk"/Tide Building**, opens to the river at high water). Sees Eleanor's gift instantly; at the preview crouches on the floor as she fixes his brief; *"There she is. There's the one who said no."* Urges her to enter the competition **under her own name**. **Function:** the door to the career life made flesh + the deepest seduction — being *seen* doing the thing she was made for (the hunger Adam can't feed). Not a romantic thread (a brief past affair w/ Ellis only, in the vision). **Anchor:** silver, unhurried; never wonders if he's wanted in a room; "use the rage before it uses you."
- **The competition (B2 engine):** Halsworth Vane open international, "individuals of note," blind-judged; a **tidal riverside site**. Eleanor's true-design temptation (rhymes w/ her Bristol tides). Ellis drafts the entry in lost time (ch3); the *submitted* application is the ch8 lost-time beat.

### Minor (ch3): Rhiannon Oke (associate at H-V, wrote her dissertation half on Eleanor's plane-tree library — the world grieving Eleanor's lost talent to her face); Faisal (2 yrs below Eleanor, now a partner there); Henry (Ellis's ex, a barrister — "two well-run countries", love-as-unbreached-frontier); Tomas (kind night nurse at Diana's care home — relays Diana's lucid line: *"tell Eleanor she doesn't have to keep the door open for me… she can shut it and go"*).
